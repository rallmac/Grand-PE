Our Streamlined Export Process

  1. Inquiry & Consultation: Understanding your specific product needs, quality standards, volume, and destination.
  2. Sourcing & Quality Assurance: Procuring products from our trusted network of farmers and suppliers, with rigorous quality checks at every stage.
  3. Documentation & Compliance: Expert handling of all necessary export documentation, phytosanitary certificates, and customs clearance.
  4. Packaging & Logistics: Ensuring secure, product-appropriate packaging and efficient, cost-effective shipping to your global destination.
  5. Delivery & Follow-up: Real-time tracking of shipments and dedicated support to ensure your complete satisfaction upon receipt.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your minimum order quantities (MOQs)?

MOQs vary by product and seasonality. Please contact us with your specific inquiry for detailed information.

Which countries do you export to?

We have extensive experience exporting to markets across North America, Europe, Asia, and the Middle East. We can discuss logistics for any global destination.

Can you provide product samples and specification sheets?

Yes, samples can be arranged for most products, subject to availability and shipping considerations. Detailed specification sheets are available upon request.

What are your payment terms?

Our standard payment terms are typically Letter of Credit (L/C) or Telegraphic Transfer (T/T). We are open to discussing terms based on order volume and client relationship.
